Alan Lyu

Biophysics · Computational Neuroscience

Hi! I am Alan Lyu (吕骏峰). I am an undergraduate student in my third year at the School of Physics, Peking University, and I am also pursuing a double degree in Biology at the School of Life Sciences, Peking University. Click here to get my CV.

My research interests are biophysics and computational neuroscience. I use methods and frameworks from physics, including information theory, stochastic thermodynamics, and dynamical equations, to understand how complex structures and behaviors emerge in living systems. Recently, I have mainly used information theory to study how living systems process information. With Prof. Chao Tang, I study how environmental signals control transitions in both directions between the sessile and motile states of Bacillus subtilis, and how this strategy can improve information gathering during chemotaxis in complex chemical environments. With Prof. Thierry Emonet, I study how the Drosophila olfactory circuit adjusts its operating point to sense both odor motion and odor gradients reliably. I am also working with Prof. Yuhai Tu to explore the physical principles of information processing in artificial neural networks.

In fall 2024, I began exploring biophysics with guidance from senior members of Tang Lab. During 2025, I took more courses in theoretical physics and the life sciences; my course list and notes are available on the Courses page. In summer 2025, I attended the QBio 2025 Summer School at Peking University, where I learned about recent work in quantitative biology. In spring 2026, I joined the Undergraduate Honors Program in Biology at Peking University to see how ideas from physics and traditional biology can inform each other. In summer 2026, I joined Emonet Lab at the Quantitative Biology Institute, Yale University for a research internship. I also greatly enjoyed the quiet pace of life in New Haven.

Outside research, I enjoy reading books about history and playing historical games. I also enjoy walking around cities and playing football. I like talking with people and hearing different ways of thinking. Please feel free to contact me!

Hi! I am **Alan Lyu (吕骏峰)**. I am an undergraduate student in my third year at the [School of Physics, Peking University](https://english.phy.pku.edu.cn/), and I am also pursuing a double degree in Biology at the [School of Life Sciences, Peking University](https://www.bio.pku.edu.cn/en/). Click [here]({{ '/assets/pdf/Alan_Lyu_CV.pdf' | relative_url }}) to get my CV.

My research interests are biophysics and computational neuroscience. I use methods and frameworks from physics, including information theory, stochastic thermodynamics, and dynamical equations, to understand how complex structures and behaviors emerge in living systems. Recently, I have mainly used information theory to study how living systems process information. With [Prof. Chao Tang](https://cqb.pku.edu.cn/tanglaben/), I study how environmental signals control transitions in both directions between the sessile and motile states of _Bacillus subtilis_, and how this strategy can improve information gathering during chemotaxis in complex chemical environments. With [Prof. Thierry Emonet](http://emonet.biology.yale.edu/), I study how the _Drosophila_ olfactory circuit adjusts its operating point to sense both odor motion and odor gradients reliably. I am also working with [Prof. Yuhai Tu](http://users.flatironinstitute.org/~ytu10/) to explore the physical principles of information processing in artificial neural networks.

In fall 2024, I began exploring biophysics with guidance from senior members of Tang Lab. During 2025, I took more courses in theoretical physics and the life sciences; my course list and notes are available on the [Courses]({{ '/courses/' | relative_url }}) page. In summer 2025, I attended the [QBio 2025 Summer School at Peking University](https://qbio.pku.edu.cn/QB2025/), where I learned about recent work in quantitative biology. In spring 2026, I joined the [Undergraduate Honors Program in Biology](https://web.bio.pku.edu.cn/UHPB/index.php?a=page&cid=28&m=Index) at Peking University to see how ideas from physics and traditional biology can inform each other. In summer 2026, I joined [Emonet Lab](http://emonet.biology.yale.edu/) at the [Quantitative Biology Institute, Yale University](https://qbio.yale.edu/) for a research internship. I also greatly enjoyed the quiet pace of life in New Haven.

Outside research, I enjoy reading books about history and playing historical games. I also enjoy walking around cities and playing football. I like talking with people and hearing different ways of thinking. Please feel free to [contact me](mailto:junfenglyu7@gmail.com)!

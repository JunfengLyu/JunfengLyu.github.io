My name is **Alan Lyu (吕骏峰)**. I am a third-year undergraduate student at the [School of Physics, Peking University](https://english.phy.pku.edu.cn/), while also pursuing a double degree in Biology at the [School of Life Sciences, Peking University](https://www.bio.pku.edu.cn/en/). In 2025 summer, I attended the [QBio 2025 Summer School, Peking University](https://qbio.pku.edu.cn/QB2025/). In 2026 summer, I joined the [Quantitative Biology Institute, Yale University](https://qbio.yale.edu/) for a research internship. Click [here]({{ '/assets/pdf/Alan_Lyu_CV.pdf' | relative_url }}) to get my CV.

My research interests lie at biophysics and computational neuroscience. I use the language and tools of physics, including information theory, stochastic thermodynamics, and dynamical equations, to understand how complex behavior, structure, and function emerge in living systems. Recently, I applied the framework of information theory to study the behavioral significance of state transitions in *Bacillus subtilis* (supervised by [Prof. Chao Tang](https://cqb.pku.edu.cn/tanglaben/)), and the principles of information processing in *Drosophila* olfactory circuit (supervised by [Prof. Thierry Emonet](http://emonet.biology.yale.edu/)). Besides, I am also exploring the low-rank structure of artificial neural networks through the perspective of statistical physics (supervised by [Prof. Yuhai Tu](http://users.flatironinstitute.org/~ytu10/)). I am always happy to discuss related ideas. Please feel free to [contact me](mailto:junfenglyu7@gmail.com).

In my leisure time, I enjoy citywalking and football :wink:

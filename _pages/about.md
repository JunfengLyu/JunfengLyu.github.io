My name is **Alan Lyu (吕骏峰)**. I am an undergraduate student in my third year at the [School of Physics, Peking University](https://english.phy.pku.edu.cn/), while also pursuing a double degree in Biology at the [School of Life Sciences, Peking University](https://www.bio.pku.edu.cn/en/). In summer 2025, I attended the [QBio 2025 Summer School, Peking University](https://qbio.pku.edu.cn/QB2025/). In summer 2026, I joined the [Quantitative Biology Institute, Yale University](https://qbio.yale.edu/) for a research internship. Click [here]({{ '/assets/pdf/Alan_Lyu_CV.pdf' | relative_url }}) to get my CV.

I am interested in biophysics and computational neuroscience. I use ideas and methods from physics, including information theory, stochastic thermodynamics, and dynamical equations, to study how living systems behave and organize themselves. Recently, I have been studying what state transitions in _Bacillus subtilis_ mean for its behavior, under the supervision of [Prof. Chao Tang](https://cqb.pku.edu.cn/tanglaben/). I am also working with [Prof. Thierry Emonet](http://emonet.biology.yale.edu/) to understand how the _Drosophila_ olfactory circuit processes information. In another project with [Prof. Yuhai Tu](http://users.flatironinstitute.org/~ytu10/), I use statistical physics to study low rank structure in artificial neural networks. If you are interested in any of these topics, please feel free to [contact me](mailto:junfenglyu7@gmail.com).

In my leisure time, I enjoy citywalking and football :wink:
